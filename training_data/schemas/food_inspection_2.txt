cube inspection
  dim  inspection.inspection_id (number) -- the unique id for the inspection
  dim  inspection.inspection_date (time) -- the date of the inspection yyyy-mm-dd
  dim  inspection.inspection_type (string) -- the type of the inspection
  dim  inspection.results (string) -- the inspection results  Pass  Pass w/ Conditions  Fail  Out of Business  Business Not Located  No Entry  Not Ready the quality is verified: Pass + Pass w/ Conditions
  dim  inspection.employee_id (number) -- the id of the employee who is responsible for the inspection
  dim  inspection.license_no (number) -- the license number of the facility that is inspected
  dim  inspection.followup_to (number) -- the id of inspections that this inspection is following up 'No data' means the inspection has no followup. if followup_to has value:  it's the parent inspection id, and this value represents child inspection id  the entire inspection: followup_to + inspection_id

  meas inspection.min_followup_to (min)
