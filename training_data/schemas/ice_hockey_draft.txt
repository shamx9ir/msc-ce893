  dim  PlayerInfo.nation (string) -- the nation of the player can ask questions about their corresponding continents. or group nations with their continents. You can refer to https://worldpopulationreview.com/country-rankings/list-of-countries-by-continent e.g.: Slovakia --> Europe

  dim  PlayerInfo.weight (number) -- the id number identifying weights

  dim  PlayerInfo.sum_7yr_GP (number) -- Total NHL games played in players first 7 years of NHL career higher --> more attendance in the first 7 years
  dim  PlayerInfo.sum_7yr_TOI (number) -- Total NHL Time on Ice in players first 7 years of NHL career higher --> more playing time in the first 7 years of career
